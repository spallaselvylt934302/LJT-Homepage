I am a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, supervised by Prof. Junxian He, who also advised me during my undergraduate studies at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU). I received my B.Eng. from SJTU in June 2024. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

## Research Interests

- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
